# Ape Economics

A decision-driven Android strategy prototype.

## v1.0
- 10 apes total, 3 directly controlled workers
- Workers generate 10 bananas each per round
- Every round presents exactly 3 choices
- Choices can improve or damage bananas, influence, popularity, violence and votes
- Banana shares provide a small risk/reward market mechanic
- Three power routes: democratic victory, corruption victory, or violent takeover
- Lose by collapsing popularity or failing to seize power before round 35

Package: `com.eugi.apeeconomics`
